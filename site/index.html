API Compliance Checker

Compare your OpenAPI definition with a DSDC Less-Than-Truckload or Full Truckload standard. It checks paths, fields, field requirements, terminology, HTTP usage, security and error handling.

Your YAML is checked in your browser. Nothing is uploaded while you work. When you run the check, the results are sent to the DSDC team. Your YAML goes with them only if you choose, and credentials and payment-card numbers are removed first. Please do not include payment card data in your specification.
